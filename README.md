## Multilingual Executive Abstracts / 六語種研究摘要

### English Abstract
Background: The transition from trial-sized dermo-cosmetic kits to full-ritual regimens represents a critical revenue inflection point for premium skincare brands entering European markets. This study evaluates consumer behavior regarding the INCELLDERM Dermatology First Package EX (Booster & Serum Set) in Italy, focusing on local currency price sensitivity and starter-to-full-ritual conversion dynamics. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 Italian consumers across Rome, Milan, and Florence assessed Likert-scale perceptions of texture, absorption, scent neutrality, and ingredient efficacy (Madecassoside, Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4, Bacillus Ferment). Cross-tabulations included skin type, income tier, MBTI personality profiles, and B2B/C-End segmentation. Results: Overall composite satisfaction reached 3.68/5.0 with a Top-2-Box rate of 65.2%. Texture appropriateness for Italian summer humidity scored highest (mean 3.93, 84.0% T2B), while Madecassoside-driven sensitive skin confidence achieved 4.01/5.0. However, EUR price fairness perception lagged at 3.42/5.0 (43.5% T2B). Notably, 77.5% initiated purchase via low-risk trial, yet only 9.5% were promoters (NPS -5.5), indicating friction in the upgrade pathway. Primary conversion triggers were visible texture improvement (95.5%) and dermatologist recommendation (86.5%), whereas high unit cost per ml (93.5%) was the dominant repurchase barrier. Strategic Implications: To optimize RIMAN Mall conversion rates in Italy, marketing must pivot from generic 'Giant BYoungPool' claims (only 42.5% trust vs. HA) toward clinical validation of Madecassoside and peptide synergies, addressing perceived value gaps against La Roche-Posay benchmarks.

### 繁體中文研究摘要
背景：從試用裝到全套療程的轉化是高端護膚品牌進入歐洲市場的關鍵收入拐點。本研究評估了義大利消費者對 INCELLDERM 皮膚科首選套裝（精華與安瓶）的行為反應，重點關注當地貨幣價格敏感度與升級動態。方法：對羅馬、米蘭和佛羅倫薩的 200 名義大利消費者進行分層調查，評估質地、吸收速度、氣味中性度及成分功效（積雪草苷、五肽、芽孢桿菌發酵產物）的李克特量表感知。交叉分析涵蓋膚質、收入階層、MBTI 人格及 B2B/C端細分。結果：整體綜合滿意度為 3.68/5.0，Top-2-Box 比率為 65.2%。針對義大利夏季濕度的質地適配性得分最高（均值 3.93，T2B 84.0%），積雪草苷帶來的敏感肌信心達到 4.01/5.0。然而，EUR 定價公平性感知落後於 3.42/5.0（T2B 43.5%）。值得注意的是，77.5% 用戶通過低風險試用開始購買，但僅有 9.5% 為推薦者（NPS -5.5），顯示升級路徑存在摩擦。主要轉化觸發因素為可見的質地改善（95.5%）和皮膚科醫生推薦（86.5%），而每毫升高單位成本（93.5%）是主要的復購障礙。戰略啟示：為優化力曼商城在義大利的轉化率，營銷必須從通用的「Giant BYoungPool」聲明（僅 42.5% 信任度對比透明質酸）轉向積雪草苷和肽協同作用的臨床驗證，解決與理膚泉基準相比的價值差距。

### Resumen Ejecutivo (Español)
Antecedentes: La transición de kits de prueba a regímenes completos representa un punto de inflexión crítico para las marcas de skincare premium que ingresan a mercados europeos. Métodos: Una encuesta estratificada de N=200 consumidores italianos en Roma, Milán y Florencia evaluó percepciones de textura, absorción, neutralidad de aroma y eficacia de ingredientes (Madecassoside, Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4, Bacillus Ferment).
