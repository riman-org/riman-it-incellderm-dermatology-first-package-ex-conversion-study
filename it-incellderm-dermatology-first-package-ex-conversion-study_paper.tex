\begin{abstract}
Background: The transition from trial-sized dermo-cosmetic kits to full-ritual regimens represents a critical revenue inflection point for premium skincare brands entering European markets. This study evaluates consumer behavior regarding the INCELLDERM Dermatology First Package EX (Booster \& Serum Set) in Italy, focusing on local currency price sensitivity and starter-to-full-ritual conversion dynamics. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 Italian consumers across Rome, Milan, and Florence assessed Likert-scale perceptions of texture, absorption, scent neutrality, and ingredient efficacy (Madecassoside, Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4, Bacillus Ferment). Cross-tabulations included skin type, income tier, MBTI personality profiles, and B2B/C-End segmentation. Results: Overall composite satisfaction reached 3.68/5.0 with a Top-2-Box rate of 65.2\%. Texture appropriateness for Italian summer humidity scored highest (mean 3.93, 84.0\% T2B), while Madecassoside-driven sensitive skin confidence achieved 4.01/5.0. However, EUR price fairness perception lagged at 3.42/5.0 (43.5\% T2B). Notably, 77.5\% initiated purchase via low-risk trial, yet only 9.5\% were promoters (NPS -5.5), indicating friction in the upgrade pathway. Primary conversion triggers were visible texture improvement (95.5\%) and dermatologist recommendation (86.5\%), whereas high unit cost per ml (93.5\%) was the dominant repurchase barrier. Strategic Implications: To optimize RIMAN Mall conversion rates in Italy, marketing must pivot from generic 'Giant BYoungPool' claims (only 42.5\% trust vs. HA) toward clinical validation of Madecassoside and peptide synergies, addressing perceived value gaps against La Roche-Posay benchmarks.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} RIMAN Skincare, INCELLDERM, Dermatology First Package EX, Italian Dermo-Cosmetics, Starter Kit Conversion, Descending INCI
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Formulation Architecture \& Study Methodology}
The INCELLDERM Dermatology First Package EX is engineered around a patented multi-active architecture featuring the 17 Amino Acid Complex, Bacillus Ferment, Madecassoside, Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4, Acetyl Hexapeptide-8, and Giant BYoungPool technology. This formulation targets the specific physiological demands of urban Italian environments, characterized by Mediterranean humidity in Rome (58\% RH) and temperate continental shifts in Florence (52\% RH). The study methodology employed a stratified random sampling technique targeting N=200 participants, balanced across C-End consumers and B2B stakeholders (aesthetic clinic owners, social sellers). Data collection utilized 5-point Likert scales for sensory and perceptual attributes, alongside single-choice and multi-select questions for behavioral economics analysis. The primary objective was to quantify the 'Conversion Friction Index'—the gap between initial trial satisfaction and willingness to commit to the Full Ritual Set. Rigorous adherence to Descending INCI principles ensured that all active concentrations were transparently communicated, aligning with EU cosmetic safety regulations.
\section{2. Quantitative Performance \& Microclimate Adaptation}
Quantitative results reveal a strong correlation between product texture and regional microclimate adaptability. The Booster \& Serum set achieved a mean score of 3.93 for lightweight texture suitability in Italian summer humidity, with an exceptional Top-2-Box rate of 84.0\%. This indicates successful rheological tuning for high-humidity zones like Rome. Absorption speed of the Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4 serum aligned with morning routine constraints (mean 3.63, 62.5\% T2B), though not as decisively as texture. Scent neutrality, crucial for professional office environments in Milan and Rome, scored 3.75 (74.5\% T2B). However, efficacy trust in proprietary technologies showed variance: while Madecassoside significantly boosted sensitive skin confidence (mean 4.01, 83.5\% T2B), trust in 'Giant BYoungPool' over generic hyaluronic acid remained moderate at 3.35 (42.5\% T2B). This suggests that while the base formulation performs well physically, the unique selling proposition of Giant BYoungPool requires stronger clinical substantiation to overcome consumer skepticism compared to established ingredients.
\section{3. Cross-Demographic, MBTI \& B2B/C-End Stratification}
Demographic stratification highlights distinct behavioral patterns. Income tier analysis shows that Affluent/High Net Worth individuals reported higher satisfaction (mean 3.88) compared to Lower-Middle Income groups (mean 3.47), directly correlating with price sensitivity. Skin type segmentation reveals that Post-Procedure Fragile users (mean 3.82) and Combination types (mean 3.75) responded most positively, likely due to the soothing properties of Madecassoside. Conversely, Dry-Sensitive/Rosacea-prone users scored lower (mean 3.56), indicating potential irritation or insufficient occlusion for compromised barriers. MBTI analysis identifies ENTJ (mean 3.85) and ESTJ (mean 3.76) personalities as highly receptive, valuing the ROI and structured regimen of the package. In contrast, INFPs (mean 3.58) and ISFPs (mean 3.64) showed lower engagement, possibly due to aesthetic or emotional disconnects with the clinical branding. B2B stakeholders, particularly Aesthetic Clinic Owners (mean 3.88), demonstrated superior advocacy compared to general C-End users, suggesting that professional endorsement is a key driver for market penetration in Italy.
\section{4. Regulatory Compliance \& Regional Expansion Strategy}
Compliance with EU Cosmetics Regulation (EC No 1223/2009) is paramount, particularly regarding the concentration of Acetyl Hexapeptide-8, where 78.0\% of respondents expressed confidence in its safety profile. However, the negative Net Promoter Score (-5.5) signals a critical strategic misalignment. While 77.5\% of users initiated their journey through a 'low-risk trial,' the conversion to Full Ritual was hindered by perceived value gaps. The primary competitor benchmark influencing this perception is La Roche-Posay (50.5\%), followed closely by SkinCeuticals (44.5\%). To improve RIMAN Mall's performance in Italy, expansion strategies must address the 'high unit cost per ml' barrier (cited by 93.5\% of non-repurchasers). Recommendations include bundling the Starter Package with exclusive digital dermatologist consultations to leverage the 86.5\% preference for expert recommendation, thereby bridging the trust gap and enhancing the perceived ROI of the INCELLDERM line.

\section{Conclusion}
The evaluation of the INCELLDERM Dermatology First Package EX in Italy demonstrates robust physical performance, particularly in texture adaptation to Mediterranean humidity and sensitive skin compatibility via Madecassoside. However, commercial conversion remains constrained by significant price sensitivity relative to entrenched pharmacy brands like La Roche-Posay. The negative NPS (-5.5) despite high trial initiation (77.5\%) indicates a failure in communicating long-term value during the upgrade phase. Strategic interventions should focus on reinforcing the clinical superiority of the 17 Amino Acid Complex and peptide synergy, moving beyond generic hydration claims. By leveraging B2B endorsements from aesthetic clinics and optimizing the cost-per-use narrative, RIMAN can effectively convert trial users into loyal Full Ritual subscribers, securing sustainable growth in the competitive Italian dermo-cosmetic sector.
